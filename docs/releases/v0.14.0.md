## Microsoft Visio drawings (view only)

- `.vsdx` files open read-only, from any `src` and from File → Open: the worker converts the drawing to a PowerPoint file in memory, a slide per page, and the presentation view shows it (thumbnails of the pages, zoom, slideshow, print). The bar shows a `VISIO` badge and no Edit button; *Download as* **PowerPoint** saves the converted file. Macro-enabled drawings (`.vsdm`) and templates (`.vstx`, `.vstm`) open the same way; a stencil (`.vssx`, `.vssm`) shows each of its masters as a slide.
- Shapes are drawn where they are on their page, through any depth of rotated and flipped groups: lines, circular and elliptical arcs, ellipses, Béziers, polylines, NURBS and splines, several geometry sections per shape (holes, parts that are outlined but not filled), rounded corners.
- A shape takes what it does not store itself from its master's shape, then from its line, fill and text styles: instances of masters keep their own size, colors and text.
- Fills (solid, see-through, gradients, the gradient patterns of older drawings), lines (color, weight, dashes, line ends), connectors along their line with their labels over a background.
- Themes: themed fills, lines, fonts and text colors are worked out from the page's theme (scheme and variation colors put through the theme's styles, connector styles of their own).
- Text is laid out by the browser in the shape's text block (place, size, angle, margins, alignment, bullets, character formats, fields), and can be selected and copied.
- Pictures (cropped to the part the shape shows), background pages under the pages that use them, hidden layers, drawings at a scale.
- A presentation has one slide size: it is the largest page's, and a smaller page is drawn as a sheet at the center of its slide.
- Not converted yet: EMF / WMF pictures (the icons of many network stencils, shown as a labeled box), shadows, exact line end shapes, tabs, hyperlinks, shape data. Visio 2003-2010 binary drawings (`.vsd`) give an error saying to save the drawing as `.vsdx`. See [features](../features.md) for the full lists.

## Spreadsheets

- Pictures placed on a sheet are shown over the cells, where they are anchored: they move and grow with resized or hidden rows and columns. A chart, and a picture in a format a browser cannot draw (EMF, WMF), is shown as a box of its size naming what is there (the chart's kind and title). Shapes and text boxes on sheets are not shown yet. Contributed in #11. Thank you.
- Number formats match Excel in five more cases: negative fractions (one minus sign), times rounded to the smallest unit shown, an empty third section showing zero as nothing, `[hh]` / `[mm]` / `[ss]` padded to their width, and fixed decimals rounding the decimal value (2.675 shows as 2.68). Reported in #3, fixed in #16. Thank you.

## Theming and icons

- Every color of the chrome is a `--de-*` design token, with fonts, radii and shadows; `theme` takes an object (`{ mode, tokens, light, dark }`) as well as `'light'` / `'dark'` / `'auto'`. `injectStyles={false}` with `@p10node/office-engine/styles.css` is for hosts that load the stylesheet themselves. See [theming](../theming.md). Contributed in #4. Thank you.
- `icons` on `DocViewer`, `mount()` and `<office-viewer>` replaces the built-in icons with the host's: a map by name, or a resolver `(name, { size })`. Contributed in #5. Thank you.

## Fixes

- Documents: a selection made from the bottom up over several pages keeps its start. Pages are virtualized, and the page the selection started on was removed once the view scrolled away from it, which moved that end of the selection.
- Collaboration: formatting cells of a shared workbook no longer leaves one participant with another style than the others after their edit was rebased over someone else's (a percentage shown as currency).

For integrators: `doc.info.from` is `'visio'` for a Visio drawing (`doc.kind` is `'pptx'`); `src` and File → Open accept `.vsdx`, `.vsdm`, `.vstx`, `.vstm`, `.vssx` and `.vssm`. `xlsx.drawings(sheet)` returns a sheet's pictures and charts with their anchors. No new dependency.
